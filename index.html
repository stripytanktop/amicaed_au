Sentence Builder

Build the sentence

Sentence Builder helps students practise putting a sentence together. They arrange scrambled words into the right order, hear the sentence read aloud, then type it out with the correct capital letters and punctuation.

Choose a level

Each level has its own set of sentences. You can come back and change level at any time.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Put the words in order

      Drag each word onto a line, or tap a word and it jumps to the next free line. Tap a word to hear it.

    2. 2

      Check your sentence

      Ticked words are in the right place. Yellow words go back to the word bank for another try.

    3. 3

      Type it out

      When the sentence is right, a typing box opens. Type the sentence yourself, then check your typing.

    Listening

    Words and sentences are read aloud using your device’s voice. Use the Audio button to turn the sound off.

    For teachers

    Open Teacher setup inside any level to swap in your own sentences, one per line. They stay until you reset or refresh the page. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

    Your sentences
