Classroom adjustments

Adjustments Finder

Start with what you are seeing in the classroom. Describe or select the barriers a student is facing, then explore adjustments and strategies you could trial.

What it helps you do

  • Begin from observation, not from a label or a diagnosis
  • Explore adjustments linked to the barriers you identify
  • Copy wording into your own planning and reviews

What we hope it achieves

More confident, better-informed adaptive teaching, with less time spent starting from a blank page and the decision always staying with you.

Any student information is kept only in your local browser session.

Notice, explore, trial, review

The planning tools follow the way good teaching already works, and sit alongside the processes your school uses.

  1. Notice

    Start with what you observe about how a student is engaging with learning.

  2. Explore

    Look at adjustments and supports that might reduce the barrier.

  3. Trial

    Try one or two ideas in the classroom and see what changes.

  4. Review

    Reflect with the student, colleagues and family, then keep, adapt or stop.

Built to respect professional judgement

Professional judgement first

Everything is a suggestion to consider. You know the student, the class and the context, and you decide what to trial.

Barriers, not labels

The starting point is what is getting in the way of learning and participation. A diagnosis is not needed to begin.

Supportive, not prescriptive

These tools support conversations between teachers, support staff, students and families, alongside specialist advice where appropriate.

These tools support, and do not replace, the professional judgement of teachers and school staff. They do not diagnose, assess or determine provision for any individual student.
